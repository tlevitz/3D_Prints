Cryo-EM Autogrid Loading Demo by tlevitz on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7190077

Summary:
Loading demo station for cryo-EM autogrids on a Thermo Fisher system. This is NOT to scale.Printed on a Dremel DigiLab 3D45 Printer, layer height 0.2 mm for big parts (body_left, body_right, clipped_grid) and 0.05 mm for smaller parts. 

Notes on assembly:
I ended up using lab tape to tape the foot to the left and right arms. Otherwise, things should clip into place. The body left + body right can be pressed together with the connectors (they may not fit perfectly depending on your printer settings, so you can use putty if the connectors have too much empty space in there, but I didn't need to). Then you should be able to put the top of each arm (already assembled with the foot) into the corresponding holes on each side of the body. This isn't the world's most sturdy demo, but it works well enough for demonstration purposes.
